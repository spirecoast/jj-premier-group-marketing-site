# Compliance requirements

These are blocking. Several are website build tasks, not vendor settings.

## 1. Call recording — Florida all-party consent

Florida requires the consent of all parties to record a communication, unlike states where one party's consent is sufficient. Follow Up Boss offers a call recording disclosure feature that plays an announcement before connecting.

**Recording must remain off until the disclosure is configured and the wording is reviewed.** Route this to counsel, not just to the brokerage compliance contact. It is not a settings question.

No part of the website build depends on this. It is listed here because enabling call recording is on the project plan and the sequencing matters.

## 2. 10DLC registration — website copy is part of the application

Business texting requires 10DLC registration, and the review checks the website. Two things must be true before submitting:

1. **The primary contact form carries texting opt-in language.**
2. **The privacy policy states that collected phone numbers are not shared or sold for marketing purposes.**

Registration also requires a valid EIN with legal name and address matching. Approval has lead time; the site copy must be live or at least final first.

### Consent language on forms

Every form that collects a phone number needs an explicit, unchecked consent control. Draft wording for legal review:

> I agree to receive calls and text messages from JJ Premier Group at the number provided, including messages sent by automated means. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Store the consent state and timestamp with the lead. Do not pre-check the box. Do not make submission conditional on it unless counsel directs otherwise.

## 3. Brokerage disclosure

Site-wide footer must carry:

- Coldwell Banker name and logo, per franchise brand standards
- Both agents' license numbers
- Office address
- Equal Housing Opportunity mark
- Realtor mark if applicable

Franchise brand standards govern logo lockup, minimum sizing, and clear space. Obtain the current brand guide rather than approximating.

## 4. Required pages

- Privacy policy, including the phone number clause above
- Terms of use
- Accessibility statement, if the brokerage requires one

## 5. MLS and IDX

Out of scope for this build. If live search is added later, display carries board rules on refresh frequency, attribution, and required disclaimers, and the data license must be obtained from the local board before any feed work begins. Lead time is significant.
