A Mass for Peace

A drumbeat. A warning. A prayer.
Widely recognized as the most performed classical work by a living composer, The Armed Man: A Mass for Peace by Karl Jenkins is a gripping musical journey from conflict to hope. Drawing on texts and musical traditions from cultures around the globe, this powerful masterpiece transcends borders, faiths, and generations with its universal plea for peace. From breathtaking beauty to thunderous drama, The Armed Man is an unforgettable concert experience that resonates deeply with our world today. General Admission Concert

Performance is approximately 75 minutes, no intermission

What’s Interesting About This Concert

  • One of the Most Performed Choral Works of Our Time
    Since its 2000 premiere, Karl Jenkins’ The Armed Man: A Mass for Peace has been performed more than 3,000 times, making it one of the most frequently performed contemporary choral works in the world. Monumental in scale, cinematic in impact, and transcendent in beauty.
  • A Musical Journey That Unites the World
    Jenkins weaves the Latin Mass with texts from diverse cultures and faiths, including the Islamic call to prayer, the Bible, English poetry, the Mahabharata, and the words of a Hiroshima survivor. The result is a deeply moving work that reminds us that the human longing for peace transcends all borders and beliefs.
  • A Powerful Message for Every Generation
    Inspired by the violence of the 20th century, The Armed Man reflects on how war has touched every culture and generation – and how peace is never guaranteed. More than 25 years after its premiere, its message remains urgent: peace is fragile, peace is precious, and each generation must choose it anew.
