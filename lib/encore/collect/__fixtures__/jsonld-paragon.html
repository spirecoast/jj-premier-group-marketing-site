October 17th – 18th

St. Armands Fall Festival of the Arts

Admission: Free

Celebrate creativity at the St. Armands Fall Festival of the Arts on October 17-18, 2026 at 434 St Armands Cir, Sarasota, Florida, USA. The festival runs Saturday from 10am-5pm, Sunday from 10am-5pm, with free admission.

Explore an outdoor gallery featuring original work by artisans from Florida and across the country, including painting, sculpture, jewelry, photography, glass, ceramics, fiber art, mixed media, metalwork, woodworking, and more.

Meet the artists behind the work, learn about their techniques and inspiration, and find one-of-a-kind pieces for your home, office, or personal collection.
