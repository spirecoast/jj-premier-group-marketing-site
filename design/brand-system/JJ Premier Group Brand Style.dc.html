Foundation
What the brand is for, and the three rules that bind it.
A move is rarely just a move. JJ Premier Group stands beside the decision. We read the market, the timing, the room, so every step forward is a step taken on purpose.
The waterline
One hairline crosses the mark, and nothing else ever crosses it. It is the brand's only graphic device, reused as a section rule, a table divider, a footer edge.
Sky is an edge
Sky never exceeds 6% of a composition and never carries text. It reads as light on water; at any larger quantity it reads as a waiting room.
Square, unshadowed
Radius 0 everywhere. Elevation is a border, never a shadow. The monogram's circle is the single exception.

What this means
All four locked JJ base colors are Coldwell Banker colors: Harbor is Slate, Sky is Carolina, Linen is Cement, Graphite is Cool Gray. The JJ palette is not a riff on the franchise palette. It is the franchise's tertiary range, promoted to lead roles and extended into full ramps. Deep Harbor #2E4A5C is the only structural color JJ added, and it deliberately sits darker and warmer than #012169 so the two never read as an attempt at the same blue. Ocean #00B4BD and Celestial Blue #418FDE stay with Coldwell Banker. Adopting them would collapse the distinction.
Open item
Franchise agreements govern how the Coldwell Banker mark may appear: permitted lockups, trademark symbols, minimum sizes and required placements. Confirm these rules against Coldwell Banker's brand center before any print run.
