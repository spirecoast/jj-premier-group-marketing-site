Rules, and what is still open
The franchise unit is front only
Coldwell Banker Realty and the “Owned by a subsidiary of Anywhere Advisors LLC.” line travel together as one locked unit, on the front, under the tagline. Neither appears on the back.
Socials are optional, icons only
Each back is drawn both ways. Where they appear it is the glyph plus the handle, never the platform spelled out. The QR always goes to the site, which is the hub for every link.
Held until confirmed
No LLC wording on any card until Joelyn confirms with Vinny. License numbers are off entirely. Compliance art is the official trademark files; Vinny signs off on co-brand sizing before the run.
