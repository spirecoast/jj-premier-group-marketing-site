# Illustration prompts · JJ Premier Group

Written for Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image). Every prompt is a complete, self-contained scene in the look of a contemporary 3D animated feature film: rich color, warm volumetric light, stylized but believable materials, and a single clear idea per frame. Sets: Encore key art, Atlas places, Tide covers, the site and brand, and a social series. No recurring characters; the coast itself is the character.

## 0 · How to prompt this model

- Describe the scene in sentences: subject, action, setting, light, mood. Add to a prompt in sentences, not keywords.
- Say what the image is for. Each prompt does.
- Semantic negatives: say what you want ("an empty promenade at dawn"), never "no people".
- Camera language: the prompts name the lens, height and depth of field. Keep that when editing.
- Text: nothing in these images is readable unless the prompt quotes the exact words and names the type.
- Ratio and size are parameters, not prose. Ratios: 1:1, 3:2, 2:3, 3:4, 4:3, 4:5, 5:4, 9:16, 16:9, 21:9. Sizes: 1K, 2K, 4K (uppercase K). Web slots 2K; hero and print 4K.
- References: up to 14 images (6 objects, 5 characters, 3 styles). Generate the style plate (prompt 1) first and attach it as a style reference to every later call so the collection stays one look.
- Iterate in the same conversation ("keep everything, warm the sky") instead of regenerating.

## 1 · The house style block

Included at the top of every prompt below. Edit it here and everywhere at once.

> A still frame from a contemporary 3D animated feature film, rendered in the warm, richly colored, emotionally lit manner of the best modern animated features: stylized but believable, soft global illumination with one strong key light, volumetric light in the air, gentle subsurface scattering on skin, leaves and fabric, physically based materials with visible texture (stucco grain, wood, linen weave, water with real refraction), shallow depth of field with soft bokeh, and a painterly, saturated but harmonious palette. The palette is the brand's, pushed rich: deep harbor navy #1e3442 and navy #2e4a5c in the shadows and night skies; amber #96702a bloomed into marigold and honey where the light falls; teal #35899c and mist #c7e7ef in water and sky; linen #f3eee6 and sand #d2c6b4 in walls, beaches and roofs; coral and rose only in sunsets and blossoms. Warm light against cool shadow in every frame. Composition is cinematic and uncluttered, one idea per frame, a generous calm area left open. Any people are small, seen from behind or in silhouette, varied, and never the subject; the coast, its light, its buildings, water and wildlife carry the scene. Nothing in the image is readable as text unless this prompt says otherwise. No logos.

## 8 · Production notes

- Generate prompt 2 first at 2K, keep the best frame, and attach it as a style reference to every later call.
- Batch by set so the light and materials stay constant; change only the scene sentences between calls.
- The prompts leave a ground for type (a bare stage floor, a calm lower third, an empty street). Extend that area in the image if you need more, never a gradient in post.
- Landmarks are described by form, never named or lettered; no image carries a logo; no image depicts a specific private home.
- Fair housing: the coast, its light and its wildlife carry every image. People, where they appear, are small, varied and seen from behind, and no image is ever edited to suggest who lives in a place.
- File keepers under `public/images/illustration/` at 2560px on the long side as JPEG, register dimensions in `lib/content/image-dims.ts`, and the site's image component serves AVIF.
