The photograph is the layout
Every section is built around one frame from the library. Type sits in the calm third of the frame and never fights it. Remove the photo and the section has no reason to exist.
One motion vocabulary
Rise, draw, fade. The hero uses all three once; cards scale 3.5% and draw a Sky rule on hover; the calendar rows slide 16px. Nothing bounces, nothing parallaxes.
The calendar is the differentiator
No other brokerage site sells a Tuesday evening. Section 06 carries the same weight as the listings because it is why anyone comes back without a house to buy.
Compliance is furniture
The MLS attribution, the Equal Housing mark and both licenses sit in the footer of every page. They are part of the design, not a legal line appended later.
