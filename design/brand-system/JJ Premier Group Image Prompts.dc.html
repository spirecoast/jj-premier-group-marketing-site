Image prompts
Every frame the system is waiting on, written to the standard of the best architectural and editorial photography rather than listing photography. Each prompt carries the lens, the aperture, the hour, the light direction, the grade and the composition, so nothing generated can drift off the brand.
The house style · in every prompt
Natural light only, the hour named. Cool shadow, warm light: the palette of Harbor blue in the shade and Linen in the sun is in the frame, not added after. Medium-format look, 50–80mm equivalent, f/4–f/8, nothing wider than 35mm ever. Straight verticals, tripod-level, no tilt. No HDR, no bracketing look, no replaced sky, no saturated grass, no halo. Grade: lifted shadows, restrained highlights, gentle warm-cool split, film-like tonal rolloff, the print quality of Architectural Digest and Dwell rather than MLS photography. Composition: one subject, generous negative space, a calm third left open for type. Nothing in frame reads as staged for a listing. No logos, no readable text. Faces: only the two agents, never a stranger.
The two agents · paste with every section A prompt
JOELYN: woman in her late thirties, straight platinum-blonde hair worn down past the shoulder, fair, composed, long features, understated gold jewelry, dresses in cream and ivory linen or a soft white blouse, the calmer of the two.
JESSICA: woman in her late thirties, wavy honey-blonde hair with warm highlights, brighter energy, quicker smile, dresses in white or ivory tailoring, occasionally a soft red that reads as an accent against linen, the sharper of the two.
Together: eye level, mid-conversation or mid-task, never posed to camera, never crossed arms, never in front of a car.
JOELYN LEFT · JESSICA RIGHT
