Before these ship
The SVGs are the editable masters: live text set in Contralto, geometry locked so the lockup holds its proportions in any fallback face. They will only look exactly right on a machine with Contralto installed, which is why each variant also ships as a 4× PNG rendered from this page for web, social and slides. The final print masters are made once: open each SVG in Illustrator with Contralto active, Type → Create Outlines, save SVG, EPS and PDF. After that the mark is a shape, not a font dependency.

Which file, when
Navy on any light ground. Reversed on Harbor 800 only. One-color when the process cannot hold two inks: embroidery, engraving, single-color screen print. White over photography and video. Black for newsprint and legal documents.
Never
Never a line on one side of JJ only. Never the name without the line. Never JJ alone above 64px. Never stretched, skewed, outlined, shadowed, or set in any face but Contralto. Never recolored outside the five inks here.
Sending to Coldwell Banker
Send the outlined EPS set plus jj-clearspace.svg. Their co-brand review needs the clearspace drawing to confirm the CB mark sits outside it at approximate equivalence.
