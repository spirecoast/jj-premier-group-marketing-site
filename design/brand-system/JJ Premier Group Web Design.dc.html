Interface rules
Navy is furniture
Header, footer, and one full-screen overlay. Everything between them is Paper or white. A navy section in the middle of a page is the tell that the system has been abandoned.
Sky is the focus ring
On screen, Sky 300 earns one job it can't do in print: the active nav underline and the focused field. It never becomes a button on a light ground.
One waterline per screen
The crossed lockup appears once — footer on desktop, contact overlay on mobile. Elsewhere the nav uses the one-line mark, which carries no rule.
No shadows, no radius
Cards are 1px Harbor 100 borders. Hover darkens the border to Deep Harbor over 120ms. Nothing lifts, nothing scales, nothing bounces.
