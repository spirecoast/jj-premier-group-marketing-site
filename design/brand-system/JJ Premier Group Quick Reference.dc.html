Ten rules that cover most decisions
1  Square corners everywhere. The only circle is the avatar crop.
2  Matte always. Gloss reads as a franchise print shop.
3  One process per piece. Never foil plus deboss plus color.
4  Navy is furniture: headers, footers, covers. Not mid-page.
5  Sky 300 is a garnish at roughly 5% of any surface.
6  Never white or Linen type on Sky 300. It fails contrast.
7  The waterline is the mark. Everywhere, at every size.
8  No gold. Satin brass is a substrate; gold foil is not in the system.
9  No emoji, no exclamation marks, in any copy anywhere.
10  Body and label text must clear 4.5:1 on its ground. Rank comes from size and weight, not from a lighter gray.
