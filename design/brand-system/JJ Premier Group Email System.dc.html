SHORT · REPLIES WITHIN A THREAD
FOUR LINES, NO IMAGES, NO RULE
After the first message in a thread, switch to the short form. A full signature repeated eight times down a reply chain is the fastest way to look like a mailing list.
Never an image signature
Blocked by default in most clients, unreadable on a screen reader, and it kills reply-quoting. Text plus one SVG logo only.
Georgia, not Jost
Web fonts do not load in Outlook. Georgia is on every machine, reads warm, and is close enough to the brand serif to feel intentional.
No tagline in a signature
It reads as a slogan in an inbox. The tagline belongs on printed and designed surfaces, not under a phone number.

Deliverability
Anything sent to more than about twenty people at once needs to go through a real sending platform with an unsubscribe link, not from a personal mailbox with everyone in BCC. Bulk sends from a personal address damage the domain's reputation for every email the team sends afterwards, including the one-to-one ones that matter most. CAN-SPAM applies to the announcements and the market letter; it does not apply to genuine one-to-one correspondence.
