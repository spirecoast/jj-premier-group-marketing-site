Installing it
Download the HTML, open it in a browser from the live site, select the signature, copy, and paste into Gmail or Outlook signature settings. Copying from the opened page resolves the photo and logo to absolute URLs, so nothing arrives as an attachment.
Why the photo works here
Real estate is a face business: the signature is often the first place a client sees who they are writing to. Square crop per the system, light-ground masters, and the Sand hairline keeps it from reading as a social avatar.
Three formats, one voice
Full for first contact, short after the first reply, plain text for phones and ticketing systems. The plain block is also exactly what a screen reader hears.

Deliverability
Anything sent to more than about twenty people at once needs to go through a real sending platform with an unsubscribe link, not from a personal mailbox with everyone in BCC. Bulk sends from a personal address damage the domain's reputation for every email the team sends afterwards, including the one-to-one ones that matter most. CAN-SPAM applies to the announcements and the market letter; it does not apply to genuine one-to-one correspondence.
