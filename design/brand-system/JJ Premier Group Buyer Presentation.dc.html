From contract to keys
Thirty to forty-five days, in order.
DAY 0
Offer accepted, deposit to escrow
A neutral third party holds it. Not the seller, not us.
DAY 1–10
Inspection, then the repair conversation
We attend. You get a written list of what matters and what does not.
DAY 10–21
Appraisal and insurance binding
In storm season, binding pauses when a system is named. We plan around it.
DAY 21–40
Loan approval, title clear, final walkthrough
The walkthrough is the day before. Bring the inspection list.
CLOSING
