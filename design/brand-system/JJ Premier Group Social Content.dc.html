Three families, one rhythm
Roughly a third each: quotes, promotions, culture. A feed that is all promotions is an advert; a feed that is all quotes is a mood board.
Culture posts sell nothing
No price, no address, no call to action, no Coldwell Banker mark. Just the wordmark at the foot, small. The restraint is the whole point.
Teal marks the series
Sky 700 belongs to the Suncoast calendar and to evening events. Seeing it in the feed should tell a follower what kind of post it is before they read it.
