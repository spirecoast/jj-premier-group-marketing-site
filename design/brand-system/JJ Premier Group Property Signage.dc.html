One message per panel
The panel says who and how to reach them. Riders say what changed. Neither says both.
Phone beats address
A passer-by can read the address off the house. Set the phone larger than everything but the brand.
Matte, never gloss
Gloss laminate blows out under afternoon sun and photographs badly in listing shots.
Sky stays off signage
Mist 200 is permitted as a rider ground. Sky 300 is not. Outdoors it turns to haze against the sky itself.
Check locally
Sign dimensions, setback and rider counts are regulated per municipality and per HOA, and brokerage identification requirements vary by state. Confirm both before a print run.
