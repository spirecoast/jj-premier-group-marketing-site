The rules that were scattered across Voice, Web, Quick Reference and the client guides, collected. Eight areas. Read this before anything is printed, posted or sent to more than twenty people.

01
Fair housing
Describe the property, never the people. Nothing that references race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status or disability, and nothing implying who a neighborhood suits. "Perfect for a young family," "safe area," "walking distance to the church" all cross the line. Say the fenced yard, the sidewalk, the distance in minutes. The Equal Housing Opportunity mark appears on every printed piece, the website footer, and every IDX page.

02
License & brokerage identification
Florida DBPR license numbers (SL…) appear on business cards, signage and email signatures. Jessica Garza: SL3658907. Joelyn Nauman: outstanding; nothing carrying her name goes to press until it is confirmed. The brokerage name, Coldwell Banker Realty, must appear on all advertising per Florida Statute 475 and franchise rules; the team name never appears without it.

03
Coldwell Banker co-brand
The CB mark is set at approximate equivalence to the JJ lockup, in CB Blue on light grounds and white on dark, with no accompanying wording, never inside the JJ clearspace, never on quote or culture posts. Vinny reviews every co-branded piece before print. Franchise identity standards supersede this system wherever they conflict.

04
MLS & IDX
Every page showing another brokerage's listing carries the Stellar MLS attribution, the listing-courtesy line, the "deemed reliable, not guaranteed" disclaimer, and the consumer-use notice. The compliance strip on the Web Design page is a component so it cannot be removed from a template. Sold data may be shown only where the MLS rules permit, with the pull date.
APPLIES TO · WEBSITE · MARKET LETTER · STAT POSTS
05
Claims & figures
Every number carries a source and a date or it does not run. No "#1," "top producer" or "award-winning" without the ranking body and year. Never predict a future price or return. Square footage and lot size from public record, not from the seller. Mortgage calculators are labeled estimates, never quotes. The seller guide's cost figures get a dated footer and a January review.
APPLIES TO · MARKET LETTER · STAT CARDS · LISTING PRESENTATION · GUIDES · WIDGETS
06
Photography, releases & testimonials
Any identifiable person in marketing needs a signed model release on file; the Moments family avoids faces for exactly this reason. Property photography is licensed to the listing unless the photographer's contract says otherwise: get print, web and social in writing before a sold home appears in a portfolio. Testimonials run with written permission and are never edited beyond trimming. Virtual staging is disclosed in the caption.

07
Email, SMS & data
Anything sent to more than about twenty people goes through a sending platform with a working unsubscribe, a physical address, and honest subject lines (CAN-SPAM). SMS follow-ups from the CRM require prior express consent captured at the form, with STOP honored (TCPA). Lead forms state what happens to the data. Never BCC a list from a personal mailbox.
APPLIES TO · MARKET LETTER EMAIL · ANNOUNCEMENTS · FUB / TWILIO AUTOMATIONS
08
Venues, partners & the culture series
Culture posts name real institutions, so they must be right: correct name, correct venue, current season, confirmed against the venue's own calendar. Never imply a sponsorship, partnership or affiliation the team does not have. No venue logo is reproduced; the institution is named in type. The posts sell nothing, which is also what keeps them clear of advertising rules.
