Before you buy a condo
Three documents to read before you buy a condo here.
01The milestone inspection report, if the building is three stories or more.
02The reserve study, and whether the reserves are actually funded.
03The last two years of board minutes. That's where the real story is.
Since the 2022 law, most buildings have to have the first two.
