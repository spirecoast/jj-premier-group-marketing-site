Selling a home you don't live in
Three things to sort out before you sell a house you don't live in.
01A key with a neighbor or with us, and a list of who's allowed in.
02The water off at the main, and the air conditioning left on. This is Florida.
03Tell your insurer the house is vacant. Many policies change after thirty or sixty days empty.
