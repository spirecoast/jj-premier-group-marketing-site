Before June 1
Before hurricane season, do these three things.
Photograph every room and the outside, for insurance. Phone photos are fine.
Look up your evacuation zone. It is not the same as your flood zone.
Find the water shutoff and know where the shutters are, before you need them.
Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. If you think someone would find this useful, please share it.
