Before you buy behind a gate
Four things to ask before you buy in a gated community.
01Who runs the gate: the association, or a district you also pay.
02What the fees cover, and what they don't. Roofs and roads are the big ones.
03The rules on renting, fences, boats and trucks in the driveway.
04Whether there's a special assessment coming. Ask it exactly that way.
